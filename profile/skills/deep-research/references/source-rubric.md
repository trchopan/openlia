# Source Rubric

Use 0-5 scores and explain unusual scores in notes.

- Authority: closeness to the primary evidence or accountable institution.
- Quality: method clarity, specificity, and reproducibility.
- Recency: fit between the source date and the research question.
- Relevance: direct usefulness for the exact claim being tested.

The helper weights these dimensions as authority 0.4, quality 0.3, recency
0.1, and relevance 0.2. The weighted score is a triage aid, not a truth score.
